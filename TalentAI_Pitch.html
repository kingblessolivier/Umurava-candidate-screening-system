How It Works

3-Stage Gemini AI Pipeline

Preprocessing extracts numeric signals first — reducing hallucination before Gemini ever sees a resume.

1 Stage

Job Understanding

Parse the job description into structured requirements: must-have skills, nice-to-have skills, experience range, education level, and role-specific scoring weights. The AI Enhance button auto-generates all of this from a rough brief.

2 Stage

Multi-Candidate Batch Evaluation

Evaluate 25 candidates per API call against the parsed spec. Preprocessing service first extracts numeric signals (GPA, years of experience, average tenure) to anchor Gemini's reasoning and prevent fabrication. Handles 50+ candidates via multi-batch re-ranking.

3 Stage

Final Ranking + Explainability

Produce a sorted shortlist with 0–100 Confidence Score per candidate, bias signal flags (gender language, age indicators, prestige bias), risk detection (unverified skills, short tenures, gaps), and a personalised "Why Not Selected?" with actionable improvement suggestions.
